These GeoJSON files were copied from `D:\SIHwork_2026\handoff\SAR_OIL_SPILL_UI_HANDOFF_FINAL` for the Detection Review map controls.

- `candidate_tile_footprints_top100.geojson`: 2019 historical training/evaluation candidate footprints.
- `qeshm_hengam_priority.geojson`: 2026 Qeshm/Hengam case-study priority candidates.
- `both_high.geojson`: 2026 case-study candidates ranked high by both model versions.
- `all_spatial_review_candidates.geojson`: 2026 QGIS spatial-review candidate layer.
- `uncertain_candidates.geojson`: 2026 candidates marked uncertain for manual review.
- `recommended_negative_candidates.geojson`: 2026 candidates filtered from the default view and available through the land/coastline debug toggle.

These layers are review support only. They do not confirm an oil spill, source attribution, or official ground truth.
